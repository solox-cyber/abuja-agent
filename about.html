Who we are

An agency built on trust.

Abuja Best Agent Housing is a real estate agency serving the Federal Capital Territory. We connect people looking for a place to live with homes to rent and buy in districts such as Maitama, Asokoro, Jabi, Wuse, Gwarinpa and Life Camp.

Renting in Abuja can feel stressful, with high upfront payments and unclear charges. Our approach is simple: show real homes, explain every cost early, and stay reachable on WhatsApp from first message to key handover.

Landlords and owners can also list with us and reach people who are actively searching.
