District guide

Where in Abuja suits you?

Short notes on the districts we list most. Tell us the one you like and we look there first.

Maitama

Abuja’s premier luxury district: embassies, quiet streets and high-end homes. Rents are the highest in the city.

Asokoro

Prestigious and secure, home to government residences and large houses with a calm, leafy feel.

Jabi

Lake, malls and limited land supply keep demand strong. Market guides put a 3-bed apartment near ₦14M a year.

Wuse & Utako

Central and busy, popular with working professionals. A 2-bed apartment in Utako is near ₦8M a year.

Gwarinpa

Nigeria’s largest single housing estate, in Phase 3. Family-friendly with the widest range of homes and prices.

Life Camp & Lokogoma

Quieter, organised and growing, with schools and modern estates at a lower price than Phase 1 districts.

District notes and typical rents are from public Abuja rent guides (2026) and are indicative only.
